FoodPeto platform

Three apps. One kitchen.
Fresh food for pets.

Customers order from a menu that changes every week, the kitchen cooks and publishes, and delivery partners bring it to the door — all connected to one live server.

🐶

Customer app

For pet parents — order fresh meals.

  • Weekly menu with day tabs
  • Pet profiles, portions & allergy alerts
  • Cart, coupons, slots & payment
  • Live order tracking with OTP
  • Refer 5 friends → 1 free meal
  • Meal-plan subscriptions
